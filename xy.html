Dodge or Die

Dodge or Die is a fast-paced 2D shooting game designed to challenge your reflexes, accuracy, and survival skills. Enemies appear from every direction, each with different attack patterns and abilities, forcing you to think quickly and stay alert.
You can move freely across the map and choose from a variety of weapons such as pistols, rapid-fire guns, explosive shots, and powerful upgrades. Power-ups like shields, extra damage, and health boosts help you survive longer during intense battles.

The game offers smooth controls and dynamic sound effects—every shot fired and every enemy defeated feels immersive and satisfying.

Vite Run

Introduction

Vait is an exciting and fast-paced 2D platformer that takes you on a colorful adventure filled with action and challenges. Run, jump, and dodge your way through beautifully designed levels, each packed with obstacles, moving platforms, hidden areas, and small enemies waiting to test your skills. As you explore each stage, you can collect coins, special rewards, and power-ups that help you progress further and achieve higher scores. The game features smooth and responsive controls, clean animations, and vibrant visuals that make the experience fun for players of all ages. Whether you’re aiming to complete levels quickly or trying to collect every last coin, Vait delivers a simple yet engaging gameplay experience that keeps you coming back for more. Your mission is simple — survive every challenge, explore each level fully, and reach the finish line with maximum rewards!
