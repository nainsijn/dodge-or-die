🎮 Our Games



1. Dodge or Die

Dodge or Die is a 2D shooter where you must survive waves of zombies using your gun.

The gameplay includes:

  • enemies from all directions
  • Shooting mechanics
  • Score-based survival


2. Vite Run

Viat Run is a 2D survival adventure game featuring a cute dog character. Your mission is simple: run and survive.

In this world, the dog must avoid:

  • Different types of deadly plants
  • Dangerous spiders
  • Fast-moving bees

Touching any obstacle reduces your lifeline. Fast-paced, smooth, and clean!
